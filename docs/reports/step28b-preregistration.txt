--- 2. the ONE common-depth diagnostic ---
  depth = 20 for EVERY source.  Chosen because 20 is the largest canonical evaluation K in the
  frozen protocol (K = 5/10/20), NOT from observed fusion performance.
  A20 = SASRec(top20) + Two-Tower(top20)
  D20 = SASRec(top20) + Two-Tower(top20) + TIGER(top20)
  Identical RRF_K=60, weights 1, dedup, tie-breaking, cohort, evaluator.
  NO other depth is attempted (no 10/30/50/100).  This is sensitivity analysis, not a search.
  The asymmetric and common-depth policies are BOTH reported; neither is selected on the
  basis of which scores better.

--- 3. artifact reuse (section 6) ---
  No retrieval, inference or training is re-run.
    SASRec / Two-Tower source heads: /tmp/step28_heads_cache.npz, 20 000 rows x depth 1000,
      produced by the Step-2.8 run (which reproduced the historical fusion control bit-exactly)
    TIGER source heads: runs/step28_tiger_heads.jsonl, 20 000 rows, min depth 20
  Top-20 is therefore an exact deterministic truncation of frozen ranked output.
  The asymmetric A/D arms are ALSO re-derived from the same frozen heads so their per-user
  ranks are available for the paired analysis; the resulting rank-array hashes are compared to
  the frozen Step-2.8 values as an integrity check.

--- 4. paired uncertainty method (PREDECLARED) ---
  Binary hit/Recall comparison: EXACT paired McNemar test
    b = A hit and D miss      c = A miss and D hit      n = b + c
    two-sided exact p = min(1, 2 * sum_{k=0}^{min(b,c)} C(n,k) / 2^n)
    reported at K = 5, 10, 20 for BOTH the asymmetric policy (A vs D) and the common-depth
    policy (A20 vs D20).  No continuity correction is used because the exact test does not
    need one.
  NDCG comparison: PAIRED USER-LEVEL BOOTSTRAP
    resample the 20 000 users with replacement, 10 000 resamples, seed 20260201
    per-user NDCG@K = ndcg_at_k(rank, K) from the frozen recommendation.evaluation.metrics
    report the observed paired mean difference and the 2.5th/97.5th percentiles as a 95% CI
  Both are uncertainty CHARACTERIZATION.  Neither is a pass threshold and neither is a new
  acceptance criterion.

--- 5. no post-hoc threshold ---
  Point estimate, uncertainty and depth robustness are interpreted SEPARATELY.  A positive
  point estimate with wide uncertainty will be described as such; no significance requirement
  is introduced after seeing the result.

--- 6. correctness gates R1-R8 (frozen before results) ---
  R1 source artifact hashes match the frozen Step-2.8 inputs
  R2 cohort 20 000 / 20 000 identical
  R3 target identities identical
  R4 common-depth truncation is deterministic
  R5 same RRF implementation (reciprocal_rank_fusion, RRF_K=60)
  R6 same evaluator (evaluate_arm -> evaluate_batched)
  R7 no target leakage
  R8 repeated ranks and metrics deterministic
  All must PASS before interpretation.
