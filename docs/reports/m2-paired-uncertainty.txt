3. METHOD (identical to the Step-2.8b preregistration)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  hit@K difference      EXACT paired McNemar, no continuity correction
                        two-sided p = min(1, 2 * sum_{i=0}^{min(b,c)} C(n,i) / 2^n)
  Recall/NDCG delta     PAIRED USER-LEVEL BOOTSTRAP
                        resample the 20 000 users with replacement, 10 000 resamples,
                        seed 20260201; observed paired mean difference with the
                        2.5th/97.5th percentiles as a 95% CI
  For a single-positive protocol Recall@K == hit@K exactly, so the exact McNemar test IS the
  test of the Recall@K difference; the bootstrap CI is reported alongside it.

  CLASSIFICATION RULE -- DECLARED BEFORE ANY RESULT WAS COMPUTED:
    SUPPORTED                at every K: McNemar p < 0.05 AND both the Recall@K and the
                             NDCG@K bootstrap 95% CI exclude 0
    DIRECTIONAL ONLY         point estimates share one sign across K, but the SUPPORTED
                             conditions are not all met
    NO DETECTABLE DIFFERENCE point estimates do not share a sign across K
    EVIDENCE MISSING         no frozen per-user evidence exists
  This rule is a REPORTING classification, not a new acceptance criterion: no accepted result
  is re-graded by it.

     exactly, so this is a reporting limitation, not an analytical gap.
