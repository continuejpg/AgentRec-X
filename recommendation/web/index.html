AgentRec-X Trustworthy Conversational Product Recommendation

Local research demo. Recommendations come from the accepted SASRec checkpoint and the accepted explicit-preference policy. When the optional language-model policy is selected it chooses only the route and which stated preference values are stored — never products, scores, evidence or ranks. Scores are raw ranking scores, not probabilities. No production authentication is provided.
